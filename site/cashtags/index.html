Post it. It's live.

No wallet to connect, no sign-up, no form. Mention the bot with a ticker and attach an image — it replies with your contract address once Solana confirms the launch.

  1. 1
    Post
    Tag @xxxdotfun: launch $TICKER, an optional name (in quotes or after the ticker) and a description after |. Attach one image.
  2. 2
    Pay
    Launches are currently free — the platform covers creation costs.
  3. 3
    Launch
    The coin is created on pump.fun and verified on-chain. The bot replies with the contract address and links.
  4. 4
    Own it
    Its token page is created automatically. Add the word community (launch $TICKER community) and it also gets a community you own — sign in here with the same X account to edit its banner, description and rules.

· The X account that posts earns 80% of the coin's pump.fun creator fees, split on-chain.

· One post launches at most one coin. Repeated mentions of the same post are ignored; the same ticker twice in 24 h is refused.

· Limit: 5 launches per X account per day.
