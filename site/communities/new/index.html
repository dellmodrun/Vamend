Official community for an existing token

Paste a Solana token's contract address. Its official community can only be opened by someone who proves they control the token — holding it isn't enough.

How verification works
  • Instant: you sign a one-time message with the token's current mint authority, metadata update authority, or the creator recorded by its launch program (e.g. pump.fun). We read these from Solana at the moment you claim.
  • Manual: when authorities are revoked or unclear, platform reviewers check your evidence. Holding tokens never counts as proof.
  • Disputes: if an official community already exists, your claim is reviewed; the current owner keeps control unless reviewers decide otherwise. Every decision is audit-logged.
