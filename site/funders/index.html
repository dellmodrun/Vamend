How fees work

  1. 1. The coin is created on pump.fun with a fee-sharing config: 80% to the X account's vault, 20% to the platform treasury.
  2. 2. Trading generates creator fees. The platform cranks pump.fun's permissionless distribution; each split is recorded with its transaction.
  3. 3. The account owner signs in with X (proving the permanent X id), links a wallet by signing a message, and claims.

Identity is the X user id, not the username: renaming the account doesn't move or lose the fees.
